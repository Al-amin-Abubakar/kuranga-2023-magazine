Special School Kuranga: Our Alma Mater History

This opening section preserves the institutional legacy of Special School Kuranga before you explore the 2023 Set digital magazine. Founded with a noble vision to provide qualitative, disciplined, and comprehensive education, Special School Kuranga has stood as a beacon of academic rigor and moral uprightness.

The Class of 2023 holds a distinguished place in the annals of the school. Throughout our years within these walls, we experienced rigorous training in leadership, scientific inquiry, sportsmanship, and unbreakable brotherhood under the guidance of dedicated mentors and teachers.
